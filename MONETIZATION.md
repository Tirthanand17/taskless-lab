# Monetization Strategy

TaskLess Lab should not depend only on ad revenue.

## Stage 1 — before platform monetization

- direct viewers to free utility tools
- convert complex/recurring problems into paid fixed-scope automation work
- release small paid templates or scripts only when a repeated audience problem is validated

## Stage 2 — audience proof

- test relevant software affiliate programs
- only recommend tools that were actually evaluated
- clearly disclose affiliate relationships
- prioritize tutorials with commercial intent but genuine usefulness

## Stage 3 — platform monetization

Apply for YouTube monetization only after meeting current eligibility and policy requirements. Do not optimize for low-quality volume or reused content.

## Revenue target

INR 10,000/week is an operating target, not a promise. The fastest plausible route is a blended model: one or two service conversions plus content-led growth, rather than waiting for ad revenue alone.
