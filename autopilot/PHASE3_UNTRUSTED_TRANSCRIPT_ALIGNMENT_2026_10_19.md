# FlowMinute Lab — untrusted transcript alignment intake

**19 October 2026 | Public Draft PR #5 | Offline text/time checks, no audio transcription**

The earlier full-media read-only H.264/AAC SHA-bound inspector and five-window final AAC waveform screen reject broken muxed streams, silence or obvious sine placeholders. Neither verifies what the actual speaker said. Authored subtitles in `phase3_voice_subtitle_handoff.py` remain **script-derived estimated cue times**, not real speech-aligned files.

New `autopilot/cloud_transcript_alignment_intake.py` defines a small typed `CandidateWordTranscript` and per-word millisecond timings. It accepts externally asserted unverified ASR/manual/synthetic metadata only and matches it against **the exact current canonical author-script SRT** for the intended English short, Hindi short or Hindi long episode. Intake refuses missing, repeated, reordered or invented words; SHA-256 of SRT or previously observed MP4 mismatch; wrong episode/language; invalid whole-episode time bounds; overlapping, out-of-order or excessively long word intervals; and word boundaries shifted by >900ms outside their source draft cue. It additionally crossbinds the original `TechnicalFullHDObservation` and `MuxedAudioScreen` to the exact MP4 SHA and denies a failed WAV/AAC screen or any falsely asserted positive release flag. Unicode NFC comparison respects Devanagari vowel/combining marks. Candidate evidence is not stored, uploaded or sent to an external ASR model.

This is **strictly a consistency check of user/subsystem-supplied text against other supplied script/timing fields**, not independent acoustic evidence. Any dishonest caller can synthesize a matching word list without speaking. Even a perfect three-language-lane shape report therefore asserts `actual_audio_words_verified=false`, `spoken_word_subtitle_sync_verified=false`, `spoken_language_verified=false`, `ASR_or_human_transcriber_identity_authenticated=false` and `publishing_authorized=false`. Genuine word timestamps require an approved independent ASR/human listener with audio bound to current SHA, separate Ed25519 trust roots and externally signed rights/editorial checks. A future signed final-output receipt must not reuse the already existing Phase 4 publishing ledger or conflate it with this draft structural result.

10 new no-network tests use **synthetic transcripts built from the already authored draft SRT** (not spoken words). They check good three-lane structural matches preserving all safety vetoes, missing/extra/changed/reordered Hindi/English words, timing drift/overlap, wrong language/SRT/MP4 hash, AAC screen failures and attempted privilege escalation, Devanagari glyph substitution, incomplete or forged three-lane results, stale SRT, and malformed/unbounded word counts. Tests are added to existing Cloud Scout and full quality gate, without new workflows, narration generation, audio files, downloads or paid services.

Real Hindi+English natural separate voices, licensed clips, verified ASR word alignment, complete HD video rendering and capacity, independent human reviews, zero-cost private storage, Google OAuth publishing and channel analytics remain production blockers. The original **21 production acceptance subphases across six unfinished main phases** remain OPEN; public PR #4 and #5 remain DRAFT/unmerged. No Nirala/laptop, external provider connection, production database changes, channel upload/scheduling or charges; ₹0/month recurring limit unchanged.
