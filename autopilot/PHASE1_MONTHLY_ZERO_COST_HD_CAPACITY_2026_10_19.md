# FlowMinute Lab — ₹0 native-HD monthly cloud-capacity admission analysis

**19 October 2026 | Public Draft PR #5 only | No deployment, account connection or upload**

This offline `autopilot/cloud_capacity_admission.py` makes the **2 Shorts + 1 long video per day** promise quantitatively testable without claiming that a one-second synthetic HD smoke clip proves a complete video production pipeline. A 30-day period is **60 original 42-second Shorts plus 30 original 432-second longs**, 90 distinct deliveries, **15,480 encoded media seconds at native HD and 371,520 video frames at 24fps** (before multilingual duplicate uploads, changes, re-renders or previews).

The explicit three-lane evidence contract requires one **actual full-duration** H.264/AAC trial each for English Short, Hindi Short and Hindi long; exact native frames/dimensions, actual wall-clock render time, measured bytes/peak RAM, full-stream decode, narration/caption technical QA, separate rights QA, and a test timestamp not more than 14 days old. The calculator extrapolates ONLY AS **UNVERIFIED HYPOTHETICAL NUMBERS**, adding 25% capacity headroom and two private file copies. Unknown or incomplete results are blocked. Provider allowances also require a reported zero base recurring monthly cost, explicit hard overage cutoff, a future validity date, verified private durable media storage, enough monthly render seconds, enough RAM, and sufficient retained bytes. These inputs remain untrusted caller assertions and cannot authorize the project or prove license rights.

Official platform references checked on 19 Oct 2026:
- Cloudflare Workers Free CPU limit is **10 milliseconds per request/cron** with 128MB memory; not a native HD codec/render host: https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/
- Cloudflare D1 Free-tier query limits are enforced and D1 ceases queries on exhaustion rather than providing unbounded writes: https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/product/d1/
- GitHub public standard hosted runners are documented as free, with 4-vCPU/16-GB Ubuntu runner shapes, but repo CI access alone **does not provide a suitable private OAuth/video storage/publishing identity**: https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/runners/github-hosted-runners

The admission classifier has an explicit blocker for Cloudflare Workers Free as a renderer and another for public GitHub CI as an upload/OAuth/media store. The hypothetical "private-verified-zero-overage-worker" case is deliberately *not* an account/plan claim and yields `HYPOTHETICAL_RESOURCE_FIT_NOT_AUTHORIZED` even when every supplied number fits. **All outputs always have `cloud_deployed=false`, `publishing_authorized=false`, `youtube_upload_authorized=false`, `independent_provider_authority_verified_by_software=false`**. There is no cost API, live cloud query, account creation or automatic external deployment.

11 strict no-network unit tests cover baseline workload, missing evidence, hypothetical finite fit without permission, rejection of one-second sample laundering, Cloudflare free CPU limit, public CI/private data mismatch, nonzero overage/invalid offers, compute/storage/RAM shortfalls, license/narration/audio/freshness and malformed/duplicate/non-finite measurements.
