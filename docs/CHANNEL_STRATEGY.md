# Channel Strategy

## Public brand

**FlowMinute Lab**

Tagline: **Less repetitive work. More useful output.**

The public identity is brand-only. No personal identity is required in videos, channel art, descriptions, metadata or calls to action.

## Positioning

Practical, faceless tutorials showing how AI, spreadsheets and Python can remove repetitive work.

The channel is not an AI-news channel and not a generic “make money with AI” channel.

## Three content pillars

1. **AI productivity workflows (50%)**
   - real tool demonstrations;
   - files, research, writing, analysis and repetitive office work;
   - compare AI output with a clear before/after.

2. **Excel / CSV / Power Query (30%)**
   - data-cleaning mistakes;
   - repeatable transformations;
   - import/export reliability;
   - reporting workflows.

3. **Python automation (20%)**
   - small scripts with a visible business outcome;
   - multi-file processing;
   - validation/testing;
   - APIs and report generation.

## Format strategy

- Shorts are discovery: one problem, one fix, one payoff.
- Long-form is search/watch time: 4–10 minute complete workflows.
- Winning Shorts become long-form tutorials.
- Winning long-form moments become new Shorts.

## Publishing cadence

Start with 4 Shorts + 1 long-form per week, not daily spam.

Increase only when:
- quality gate remains green;
- episode substance stays materially different;
- analytics support the extra volume.

## Revenue design

Do not wait for ad revenue alone.

Potential later layers:
- YouTube Partner Program;
- carefully disclosed tool affiliates;
- brand-owned workflow templates/tools;
- sponsorships that match the audience.

No affiliate account, payment account, AdSense account or sponsorship contact is configured until separately authorized.
