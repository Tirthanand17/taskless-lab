## Official YouTube findings (verified public source documents)

1. YouTube frames performance in **appeal** (choose to watch), **engagement** (stay), **satisfaction** (enjoy/return). An original video whose first second promises nothing tangible will lose viewers even when an encode is technically perfect: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650
2. For Shorts, inspect **Shown in feed**, **stayed to watch vs swiped**, **engaged views**, absolute and relative watchtime, shares, comments, subs; for long inspect thumbnail **impressions CTR** and first-30s retention: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314355 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415
3. YouTube states it does **not require daily uploads**; precise publish hour isn't known to determine long-term watch performance. Never prescribe "three times a day" as an algorithm hack: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805
4. Original Shorts prioritization announced **October 1, 2026**; small edits of recycled layouts without a distinct original educational contribution are insufficient in spirit, and republishing others' clips gets reduced reach: https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts
5. Since **August 24, 2026**, public views start when playback begins across formats; **engaged views** is essential for evaluating quality rather than just play starts: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785
