- **16:00 IST — Megabits and megabytes.** Twenty scenes with an original eight-bit tile, decimal/binary unit comparison, packet conveyor, and progress/timer graphics. Demonstrates ideal 100 MB / 20 Mb/s = 40.00 s vs 100 MiB / 20 Mb/s = 41.94 s. Calls out network overhead, congestion, server variation and idealized assumptions. The long narrative is currently a multi-scene estimated-duration **script/plan**, NOT a completed voiced long-form video.
- **20:30 IST — Earthquake energy scale.** Six scenes with an original rising exponential magnitude/energy column illustration; +1 magnitude ~31.6x energy, +2 magnitude 1000x, measured amplitude 10x for +1. Explicitly distinguishes energy from property damage.

All stories are original wording and have topic-specific claims-to-official-link mappings, exact Decimal computations, bounded unique beat IDs, separate vector grammars, scene word/time budgets and SHA256 canonical draft inventories.
