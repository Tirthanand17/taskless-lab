# ADR-0102 — B4h0 daily original synthetic casebook, not factual news or endless generative AI

**2026-11-07 IST | Branch 2 educational story development | NO PUBLISH**

## Verified defect
B4g8 exact-SHA and B4g9 near-repeat diagnostics were needed because the original B4d9 17-scene 4/9/4 rehearsal spoke one fixed template per each of six topic families. Merely changing the daily research lead and title did not change the voiceover. Both fingerprints **detect** recycling but cannot rewrite stories.

## Decision
Build a hand-authored, strictly local **7-case educational micro-experiment book for each of six families**, **42 original fictional scenarios** total. For every episode, a reproducible calendar day + verified selected topic family + time slot selects one complete scenario: a named learning problem, synthetic inputs, a specific two-path comparison, an imagined failure, and a reviewable corrective step. Independently rotate three educational openings to reduce identical starts. Do not insert unverified RSS titles, public article text, web claims, model guesses, source quotes, real benchmarks, licensed figures, credentials, channel data or paid API content into narration.

Existing episode binding, 4/9/4 chronological scene counts, 58s/240s initial storyboard duration targets, 30 fps math, source-claim docket validation, cinematic scene guards, PCM voice clock reconciliation, subtitle-safe zones, audio QA, rights and release protection remain unchanged. Only **unapproved** synthetic teaching examples vary.

## Limitations and non-claims
This is a **finite repertoire**, not unlimited creative generation: a storybook may repeat eventually. B4g8/B4g9 must continue to report suspected exact and near repetition; changing story titles is not proof of novel audio. Dates and scenario choices are selection keys, not factual statements. Content still does not explain any new research headline or claim verified facts. No owner signed watch, independent fact/rights approval, YouTube OAuth, scheduling or publishing is added.

## Acceptance
- Seven consecutive days of the same family/slot produce seven different *actual narrated scripts*, not only different IDs or headlines.
- Exercise titles/inputs/failures/alternatives are human-authored constants, separated from unverified research data.
- Every 4/9/4 script fits existing short/long word-duration targets, valid 30fps contiguous scene timings and >=6s per scene, while avoiding illegal news claims.
- Existing adversarial tests for tampered source docket, no source leakage, no paid calls, no release, and B4g8/B4g9 hashing remain passing.
- GitHub PR's four exact-head CI workflows must pass. After merge, check an actual main-branch **NO PUBLISH** workflow before claiming daily operation.

**Production readiness stays conservatively 45/100, 0/21 independently verified unattended public releases** until distinct human-reviewed production and YouTube evidence exists.
