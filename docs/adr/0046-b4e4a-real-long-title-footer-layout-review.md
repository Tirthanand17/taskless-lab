## Concrete fix, no new facts or licensing claims

`autopilot/cinematic_editorial_grid_v5.py` replaces only **top source title/brand/metric placement and legacy source footer** on the opt-in cinematic mode. It does not alter original science/number `display_title` and `display_value` or the narration/SRT/thumbnail/right agreements. Exact source content is cryptographically checked against the immutable NASA/NIST/USGS drafted storyboard. It draws measured DejaVu original typography and hard-fails if a title cannot fit 1–2 lines in its reserved box. All brand, chapter, title, metric and review footer rectangles are checked against one another. Its separate topical palette faithfully blends to the original root SVG background to avoid discontinuities.
