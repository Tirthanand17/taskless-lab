# TaskLess Lab — YouTube Research (2026-10-02)

## Decision

Position the channel around **AI productivity + Excel + practical automation**.

This is broader than an Excel-only channel, but narrower than generic AI news. Every video must demonstrate one concrete workflow, problem, or before/after result.

## Demand snapshot

Current vidIQ research used for this decision:

- `ai tools`: ~740,949 estimated monthly searches; competition 56.4/100.
- `ai automation`: ~1,081,782 estimated monthly searches; competition 63.9/100.
- `free ai tools`: ~171,519 estimated monthly searches; competition 41.9/100.
- `excel tips`: ~48,897 estimated monthly searches; competition 39/100.
- `data cleaning in excel`: ~26,217 estimated monthly searches; competition 26.8/100.
- `csv cleanup`: ~5,024 estimated monthly searches; competition 8.2/100.
- `python automation`: ~32,835 estimated monthly searches; competition 43.8/100.
- `python automation tutorial`: ~4,632 estimated monthly searches; competition 25.6/100.

## Competitive pattern

Faceless AI/productivity channels are currently capable of rapid growth, but the useful pattern is not generic slideshow content. Breakout videos tend to:
- promise one clear outcome;
- show the tool/workflow immediately;
- use short, high-contrast packaging;
- demonstrate the result rather than only describing it.

## Content mix

- 50% AI productivity workflows with real demonstrations.
- 30% Excel / CSV / Power Query problem-solving.
- 20% Python automation and repeatable workflows.

## Packaging rules

- First frame must visually deliver the title promise.
- Shorts hook: target the first 1–2 seconds.
- Long-form: no logo intro; demonstrate the final result early.
- Thumbnail: one idea, 2–5 words, high contrast, before/after or mistake/fix concept.
- Titles: searchable when demand exists; curiosity-driven only when the visual explains the promise.
- Tags are secondary. Title, thumbnail, idea, retention, and satisfaction are primary.

## Monetization guardrail

Do not mass-produce templated clips. Each episode must materially differ and provide distinct educational value. The long-term revenue model is blended: YouTube monetization + later sponsorship/affiliate/product opportunities + brand-owned tools, but no revenue claim is guaranteed.
