# FlowMinute Lab Monetization Path — October 2026

## Current YouTube thresholds

As of October 2026, YouTube's standard ad-revenue entry path is:
- 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified public long-form watch hours in the last 12 months; or
- 1,000 subscribers + 10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days.

Shorts-feed watch time does not count toward the 4,000 long-form watch-hour requirement.

Expanded YPP / earlier fan-funding and Shopping access can begin at:
- 500 subscribers;
- 3 valid public uploads in 90 days; and
- either 3,000 qualified long-form watch hours or 3 million qualified Shorts views.

YouTube has announced that starting 1 February 2027, new applicants for ads/Premium revenue will need:
- 1,000 subscribers + 8,000 qualified long-form watch hours; or
- 1,000 subscribers + 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days.

The 500-subscriber expanded-YPP thresholds are not changing in that announced update.

## Channel strategy consequence

FlowMinute Lab should not rely on Shorts alone.

Cadence target:
- Shorts: discovery and topic testing.
- Long-form: searchable evergreen tutorials and qualified watch hours.
- A successful Short should point toward a deeper long-form tutorial when relevant.

## Revenue realism

Do not model ad revenue as immediate income. The channel must first build organic views, subscribers and qualified viewing time, and it must pass YouTube's policy review.
