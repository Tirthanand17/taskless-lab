## Concrete creative improvements to increase engagement responsibly

1. Hook first 1–2 seconds: question and realistic human scene, NOT logo bumper or generic "Meta just announced..." blank screen.
2. Distinguish free vs paid, developer API vs consumer rollout; avoid inaccurately claiming user can enable Wonder in WhatsApp or Instagram today.
3. Show an actual personal problem visually—trip plan and budget—then use animated memory notes to explain a concept. Focus is a step-progress/error recovery example; must be labeled hypothetical.
4. Avoid 10–15-second nearly static title cards; make b-roll, scene transitions, panel-level movement and mobile legibility support the explanation.
5. End with ONE easy, topic-specific comment question. Never request fake views/comments or repeated low-value likes.
6. Thumbnails: large, phone-legible "AI KNOWS YOUR PAST?" and one right-hand example, no false screenshots of actual Meta AI.
7. Studio: Once Long 010 is published, set it as the **Related video** on both Shorts in native Studio controls; YouTube Data API video scheduling alone cannot attach the related-video link.
8. A/B test the next 3–5 thumbnails/titles ONLY after enough impressions for meaningful conclusions. Track Shorts engaged views/stayed-to-watch, long CTR and average view duration, comments per 100 engaged views and subscriber gain.
9. Synthetic conversational narration with no claims of human recording; technical verification on audio clips. Human listening review should remain explicitly reported as unobserved if the owner approves without listening.
10. Source links, stock video rights and AI product availability warnings go in descriptions. Final captions must be aligned before upload; unreviewed machine-estimated caption timing is not accepted.
