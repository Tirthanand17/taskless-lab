## Decision
Freshness: Google announced its new Gemini universal agent at Gemini at Work on October 8, 2026. A work coworker can have a dedicated identity and can use company-authorized context and tools. News relevance is strong; YouTube demand and future views are **not guaranteed**.

## Verified facts / limits
Source: https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/welcome-to-gemini-at-work-2026
- Announced October 8, 2026.
- Agent can handle knowledge work, coding, tools, and Google Workspace context in an enterprise setting.
- Google's announcement describes dedicated coworker identities with their own email addresses and controlled access.
- This is **not** a claim that any Gmail user can activate the feature now or that any particular account has access.
- Fictional inbox/sales/report workflows are illustrative reenactments, NOT a live recording of Google's product or claims of tested integrations.

Additional context: https://www.theverge.com/tech/1007904/google-gemini-ai-agent-enterprise

## Creative quality improvements
- Begin with tangible problem or surprising claim by 0.5 seconds, no logo intro.
- Cinematic dark office-style color system with sharp UI-like workflow cards, high contrast, shallow-depth shadows, animated movement and restrained typography.
- Mobile-first vertical text, central focal action, reserve top/bottom safe regions, avoid dense panels and tiny text.
- Clear approval gate, numbers tested inside visual; do not mislead about actual agent interface.
- Audio matched to scenes, no dialogue overlapping silent outro. Limit transitions to purposeful match cuts; burn concise subtitle statements.
- Long thumbnail: SINGLE question with agent icon + approval lock, no fake screenshots.

## KPIs after release
Long: impressions, CTR, first 30 seconds retention, average view duration, subscribers gained. Shorts: shown in feed, chose to view vs swipe, average percentage viewed, replays, comments per view.

## Publishing safety
Use YouTube private upload, review checks and thumbnail, schedule each item once at intended local time, verify Studio receipts and IDs, then write state/scheduled/*.json. Do not claim completion from a rendered MP4 or an intention to schedule.
