# Branch 1 — B1v7 quality redesign and pilot validation (24 Nov 2026)

## Evidence and root cause
Studio baseline (26 Oct–22 Nov): Shorts stayed to watch 20%, swiped away 80%, 963 views and 157 engaged views; regular videos 736 thumbnail impressions, 3.9% CTR, 115 views, 1:06 average duration. These confirm a viewing-choice bottleneck and low exposure, not an exact scene-level causal diagnosis. B1v6 contact-sheet review revealed repetitive branded panels, substantial empty space, illustrative photographs not demonstrating the claimed mechanism, long explanatory headers and decorative motion. Synthetic narration and narrow topic appeal are hypotheses requiring experiments. Today's B1v6 release remains unchanged.

Research: YouTube recommendations https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650 ; Shorts discovery https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225 ; hook/mini-story https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/ ; retention https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415 ; Mayer 2021 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jarmac.2021.03.007 ; segmenting meta-analysis https://doi.org/10.1007/S10648-018-9456-4 ; Brame https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5132380/ . Learning-design evidence supports clearer explanations, not guaranteed views. YouTube's monetization policy cautions against generic repetitive mass-produced content: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 .

## B1v7 requirements
One audience, practical question, research and truthful payoff; show problem/result within first second without logo intro; every scene must have a spoken claim, matching visual action, focal point and learning purpose. Use actual captures or footage as evidence, labeled diagrams only for mechanisms, no generic stock as proof. Crop irrelevant space, change compositions when idea changes, reserve Shorts UI-safe zones, use plain spoken language, short captions, low background audio, purposeful sound effects. Long videos start with the result and deliver the title/thumbnail promise; progressively demonstrate steps, explain why, provide a practical action. No fake benchmark or unmeasured claims.
