## Findings
1. Primary confirmed Shorts bottleneck: high swipe rate. Evidence of distribution exists; no evidence of shadowban. Exact scene causing dropoff not proven; test opening.
2. Primary confirmed long-form bottleneck: low impressions; CTR 3.9% leaves room to improve but within official common range, not sole cause. Search-dependent. No detailed retention conclusions.
3. Topic fit hypothesis: best examples Incognito privacy 126, ONLYOFFICE AI spreadsheet 114, India AI websites 113, ChatGPT transcription 91, Excel date error 67, airplane mode 66, Chrome permissions 64. Recent narrow tutorials 0–5 views. Small observational sample; age and reach confound.
4. Creative review: repeated template/card aesthetic, small text, generic stock illustrations, synthetic narration, static introductory title panels; motion and music alone do not guarantee appeal. B1v6 improves imagery/audio but still retains card composition. Real screen/physical demonstrations and payoff-first hooks are next.
5. Audience promise: broad mix of Hindi/English, AI news, software tutorials, Python and consumer tech. Language metadata absent on 58/102 public uploads; metadata omission is not established as the reason for low views. Need audience/geo data before language choice. Avoid mixing entertainment CLIVOX with FlowMinute.
6. Volume does not guarantee growth. Technical release QA is already strong; measure outcome before expanding automation. No evidence of policy penalty. Repetitive mass-produced content is a monetization risk independently of reach.
