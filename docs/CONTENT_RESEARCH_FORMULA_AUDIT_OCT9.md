4. Excel users discuss formula-completion selecting wrong ranges: https://www.reddit.com/r/excel/comments/1tvjpkn/excel_autofill_of_formulaes/
5. YouTube documents appeal/engagement/satisfaction and strong early hooks: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650?hl=en
6. YouTube recommends readable, accurate, uncluttered custom thumbnails, with thumbnail experiments and retention analysis: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415

## Production guardrails
- Every dollar figure and business example is explicitly fictional and illustrated; never imply real loss or that actual Copilot suggested these exact formulas.
- The worksheet is an **illustrative recreation**; do not pretend it is a live Copilot or a direct software capture.
- Start within two seconds with a concrete, readable mismatch on screen.
- Animate the actual referenced cells and numeric result; do not produce a slideshow of large headline cards.
- One focal comparison at a time; fixed outer margins and consistent font scale.
- For Shorts use mobile-safe lower captions and avoid title/subtitle collisions.
- The long-form custom-thumbnail asset contrasts "$18,900" and "$22,500" with short text "AI GAVE THE WRONG TOTAL" but the video itself explains this is a fictional audit scenario.
- Upload a custom thumbnail only if Studio permits it without additional personal/phone verification. Otherwise use the strongest frame and do not claim the custom asset is active.
- Render, narration-duration test, contact-sheet inspection, private upload, checks clean, Studio-native schedule, real ID, and GitHub receipts are all required gates.
- Do not create duplicates. Update exact scheduled video IDs and verify eventual public playability.
