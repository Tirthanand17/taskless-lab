Excel just broke one of its oldest rules.

For decades, one cell basically meant one value.

In Excel Beta, a single cell can now hold a real list — like three project owners — not just comma-separated text.

Excel can treat those items separately, filter them, and even spill the values back out for calculations.

Microsoft is also adding functions like HAS and FLATTEN for this new array-in-cell model.

It is still a preview, so don't rebuild your production workbook yet.

But this changes what an Excel cell can be.
