Stop. This date can mean two different days.

Zero-three slash zero-eight slash twenty-twenty-six is March eighth in U.S. month-day order, but third August in day-month order.

When a CSV is imported with the wrong locale, the date can be interpreted incorrectly.

Don't fix that by only changing the cell format.

In Power Query, right-click the date column, choose Change Type, then Using Locale. Set the type to Date and choose the locale used by the source file.

Then verify the day and month.

Formatting changes appearance. Locale changes interpretation.
