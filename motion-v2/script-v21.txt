Meta just announced an AI that can remember what matters from previous conversations. But does that mean your Meta AI app has it today? Not necessarily. The new Muse Wonder models feature what Meta calls Active Memory. Imagine planning a trip today, and asking about your preferences next week. The idea is to bring relevant details back, instead of starting from scratch. That's Meta's stated capability, not a test we ran. Wonder is available to developers through the Meta Model API and Muse Code. Wider Muse access is coming later, starting with subscribers. Would you enable long-term AI memory? Comment YES or NO.
