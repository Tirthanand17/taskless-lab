Could an AI agent delete your files? Here is the part most people miss. A chatbot replies. But an agent may request actions through connected tools. Imagine it asks to delete a sample folder. That is a request, not proof it can. Permissions matter. Read-only access cannot delete files. Sensitive actions may need approval. Deny the action. Your demo files stay untouched. Remember: least privilege. Preview changes, review permissions, and keep backups.
