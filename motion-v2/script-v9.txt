This Excel total looks right. But it is missing three thousand six hundred dollars. Five invoices are visible, yet the formula stops one row early. It adds four rows and returns eighteen thousand nine hundred. Include the fifth invoice and the real total is twenty-two thousand five hundred. A formula can be syntactically perfect and still answer the wrong question. When Excel or Copilot suggests a formula, check the entire range, not just the result. Then verify against the raw records. This is an illustrative example, not a real financial incident.
