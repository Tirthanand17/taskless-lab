Claude can now help edit a Google document, spreadsheet or slide deck. But before you install it, know the catch. Anthropic says the Google Workspace add-on is in beta for Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans, not every free account. Install the official Claude add-on from Google Workspace Marketplace, then open a file and choose Extensions, Claude, Open Claude. The default Ask before edits mode waits for approval. Accept all edits skips approvals for ordinary content changes, but sensitive link and access-related actions still trigger checks. It's not the same as giving Claude your entire Google Drive.
