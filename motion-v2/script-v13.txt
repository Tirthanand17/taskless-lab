Google just announced an AI coworker that can have its own work email identity. Yes, an AI role that can keep context across tasks, instead of simply answering one question. Imagine a project agent reviewing updates, checking a spreadsheet, and preparing a team reply. In our example, a human still approves the final send. This was announced on October eighth as part of Google's new Gemini agent for work. Important: this is enterprise technology, not a free feature suddenly available to everyone in Gmail.
