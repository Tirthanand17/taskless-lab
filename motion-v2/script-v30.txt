A cyber scam happens. You've got screenshots, messages, and no idea how to explain it all. A hackathon prototype called Samarthan explored using AI to organize that evidence into a complaint draft. That's interesting, but don't trust a random AI app with your OTP, password, or private evidence. And this prototype isn't the government's reporting system. In India, report cybercrime through cybercrime dot gov dot in. For financial fraud, call nineteen thirty promptly. The bigger lesson? Let AI organize a draft, not decide what's true.
