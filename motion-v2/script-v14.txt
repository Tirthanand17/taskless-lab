The biggest AI agent mistake may not be a wrong answer. It's a wrong action. Picture this: an agent reads an invoice, changes a sales sheet, and emails the customer. If the invoice was incorrect, that mistake spreads across three apps. Here's the safer design: read the source, propose a change, verify the numbers, and require human approval before sending anything or making a payment. Google just announced a new universal Gemini agent for work. More powerful agents need better permission boundaries. Which task would you automate, but never auto-approve?
