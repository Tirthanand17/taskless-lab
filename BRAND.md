# TaskLess Lab Brand

## Positioning

**Promise:** Less repetitive work. More useful output.

## Audience

People who repeatedly clean spreadsheets, move data between files, prepare reports, or perform small office/data tasks manually.

## Voice

- practical
- concise
- calm
- no hype
- no personal backstory
- no income promises

## Visual system

- dark neutral background
- large high-contrast typography
- simple geometric motion
- before/after demonstrations
- no human face required

## Standard CTA

"Save this for the next messy spreadsheet."

For videos tied to a working free tool:

"Need the repeatable version? Link in description."
