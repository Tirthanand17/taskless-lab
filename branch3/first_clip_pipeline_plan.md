Raw starts don't guarantee meaningful attention. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281
- Twitch permits streamers to enable **sharing of their clips** to YouTube through the native feature, but platform settings do not necessarily grant separate redistribution, modification or commercial rights to a separately downloaded clip. Verify actual creator authorization for the intended edit. Source: https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/clips-settings

## Short editorial release recipe (starting *hypothesis*, not algorithm promise)
- 0–2 s: original fast readable hook with a clear promise; no misleading clickbait.
- 2–6 s: minimal verified context; label speaker/stream accurately.
- 6–20 s: precisely permitted edited source excerpt with legible captions.
- 20–34 s: meaningful original take (analysis / narrative / commentary / explanation); not bare karaoke captions.
- 34–40 s: satisfying payoff and specific relevant question, avoid empty engagement bait.
- Length is a test variable; adapt to actual source. 9:16 1080×1920, 48-kHz AAC, readable mobile subtitles, safe margins, no auto-looping spinner/circle overlay, and no unlicensed background audio.
- On-screen paid-promotion disclosures and platform paid-promotion flags whenever campaign terms/law require them.
- Source credit link and required tags in description; no invented official relationship.
- Keep **Private** default and never schedule without signed release approval.
