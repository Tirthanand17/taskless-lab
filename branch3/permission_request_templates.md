# Clivox — Rights-holder permission and campaign-operator verification

Use only verified official contact routes. Do not email a guessed address. Do not send outreach automatically without the user's approval or without a source-specific verified contact. Preserve any private responses/contracts outside the public GitHub repository; commit a sanitized permission reference and SHA-256 fingerprint if needed.

Clivox editorial team

## Campaign-specific inquiry

2. Are all underlying music, broadcaster/game publisher and third-party clips licensed for the proposed use?
3. Does permission activate only after joining/approval? May the participant keep the video live after the campaign ends?
4. Do your rules allow independently produced commentary and what footage/timestamps can we use?
5. What audience-geography, paid-promotions, source attribution, post duration, sponsorship disclosure and draft approval requirements apply?
6. Is Clivox eligible with no prior YouTube audience history?

Please send the authoritative rules or rights-holder public policy. We will not publish without appropriate authorization.

Thank you.

## Specific red flags
- Public campaign listing is NOT proof of fully granted rights.
- Twitch clip-sharing toggle does not automatically establish broad re-editing and YouTube monetization rights.
- Having creator permission for a stream does not grant game publisher, guest, broadcast and music rights.
- Campaign requiring 60%+ US audience is not a match for an unproven brand-new channel.
- A campaign listing only TikTok/Instagram is NOT Clivox YouTube permission.
- No bulk creator outreach, spam, or private data in GitHub.
