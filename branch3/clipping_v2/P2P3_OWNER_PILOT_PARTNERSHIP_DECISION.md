## Specific pilot offer to negotiate — NOT a binding contract

**Concept:** up to **three 25–55 second Shorts**, edited only from original, explicitly identified and licensed streamer-provided gameplay or livestream masters. First create an **offline/private** review; don't download public YouTube/Twitch videos through arbitrary websites or publish immediately.

CLIVOX's original contribution would include a contextual hook, accurate and independently checked quote/gameplay moment, fresh original CLIVOX explanation or analysis, purposeful editing, safe 9:16 framing, accessible captions, a clear payoff and creator attribution as agreed. Generic continuous zooms, text-only overlays, recycled meme sound effects and repeatable template clips **do not qualify** as our editorial standard.

Proposed starting terms to discuss (not accepted by either party):
- **Who owns what:** legal rights-holder's verified identity, original video/channel and source file, exact authorized clips/timestamps; separately identify relevant game publisher, guests, music, brands and platform restrictions.
- **Where/why:** explicit commercial derivative redistribution and monetization for CLIVOX channel ID \`UCfPEvy0SefE_5nNlYlY_CyQ\` on YouTube Shorts, and permission to create commentary, captions, framing and sound edits. No implied right to Twitch broadcasting or other third-party platforms.
- **Limits:** requested three private-first pilot edits, 90-day negotiation starting term, exact territory/format, promotion rights, attribution/credits, excluded songs/guests, and per-source revocation.
- **Approvals:** actual creator/authorized agency plus CLIVOX owner review the **exact final hashed video** and factual quote context before any upload. Human review cannot be replaced by AI-generated consent text or a generic platform clip setting.
- **Business:** whether flat fee, revenue split, zero-fee trial or no agreement is acceptable; decide actual dollars, payment timing, tax, invoices, rights/revenue audit and takedown/claims dispute SLA directly with the real parties. No fixed or promised earnings are invented.
- **Secure delivery:** after signed rights agreement and private-vault approval, creator directly supplies authorized original master; no source download, personal contract or OAuth token in public repo/GitHub CI.
- **Termination:** signature validity, scope, expiry, real revocations and game/music rights rechecked for each published clip; future uploads blocked immediately on grant revocation or dispute.

**Technical proposal is not legal advice or legal permission.** Actual parties should review/execute binding agreements privately. The repository's P3 30-clause questionnaire remains strictly unsigned until human authentication.
