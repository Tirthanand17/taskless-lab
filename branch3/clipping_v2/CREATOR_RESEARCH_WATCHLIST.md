## Why target famous creators at all?

Their long streams, comedy challenges, unexpected reveals and high-stakes gameplay can teach the editorial team what viewers find entertaining. Huge reach, however, makes direct commercial license negotiation harder, may already be served by official clips channels, may include celebrity guest/music/game rights, and does not guarantee that another publisher's Short will succeed. Use this list for **content research and potential rights-holder outreach**, not as a source for unauthorized copying.

## Which creators should we *actually* try to partner with first?

**Best initial commercial-fit hypothesis: mid-size (roughly tens of thousands to low millions followers) gaming/livestream creators actively seeking a clip editor or Shorts distribution partner.** This is an **outreach selection strategy**, not a claim that any named famous person licenses their content.

**Why:** They are more likely to need editorial assistance, can potentially deliver original video masters/direct source access, may respond to direct partnership proposals and allow a simple, properly scoped revenue-share trial. Size alone does not prove accessibility or permission.

### Initial eligible partner prospect criteria

1. Public business collaboration channel explicitly offered by the creator (not a scraped personal address); clear creator-brand identity and reasonable prospect of legitimate contract.
2. Regular *long* owned video or stream archives and consent to a 3-clip **private test**, plus permission for exact video segments and original commentary.
3. Source must be free of third-party licensed background music, protected TV/film content, unreleased game assets, guests without clearance, and unpermitted platform capture.
4. Creator willing to deliver original source file directly to the approved **private** CLIVOX asset system, or appoint CLIVOX as an authorized platform editor with applicable scopes and commercial reuse rights.
5. Explicit written terms about ad monetization, attribution, exclusivity, campaign expiration, region, joint copyright, takedown, future sublicense and ownership of CLIVOX-added narration.
6. Source has actual entertaining moments the editor can distinguish by event/payoff, not mere creator fame.
7. No children used for ridicule, harassment or protected-group targeting; original context must be accurate.

**Prospect selection should prioritize people willing to grant scoped rights, not fame alone.** Famous-list names remain market/format research when there is no direct contract.

## Research order and editorial lanes

| Lane | Proposed starting priority | What to detect | Special warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gaming clutch, near miss, unexpected comeback | FIRST after grant | Turning point, gamer commentary, reactions, payoff | Embedded publisher rights, in-game songs, truthful gameplay titles |
| Streamer jokes and reactions | FIRST after grant | Authentic laugh, surprise, reversal, funny chat | Consent from guests, harassment, humiliation and copyright music |
| GTA VI official/approved game streams | POST-LAUNCH ONLY | Novel interaction, physics, mission twist with analysis | Official Rockstar release 2026-11-19; no leaked pre-release material or unverified "gameplay" |
| Vlog/challenge moment | SECOND | Setup→stakes→reveal; original narration commentary | Sponsors, bystanders and incidental identifiable people |
| Podcast/interview insights | LATER | 1 complete insight, explained and contextualized | Accurate quote, reputational risks, guest rights and clip-source restrictions |

### Partnership sequencing (no actual outreach conducted)

1. Discover 10 potential *mid-size* creator-owned long-form archives using compliant public channel references, without storing platform-derived scoring or public video binaries.
2. Verify each creator's **public business inquiry route**, actual authority and whether they invite editors/clip partnerships; do not collect private contact data in Git.
3. **Human owner approves** concise outreach offer: 3 privately reviewed Shorts for creator consideration; define that nothing posts before separate license, final human preview and explicit owner sign-off.
4. If the creator wants to collaborate, obtain/verify an actual commercial YouTube grant and permitted source-delivery method before any asset processing. A licensing lawyer or knowledgeable operator may need to review unusual terms or content restrictions.
5. Select one stable, high-clarity lane for pilot; validate whether legitimate audience feedback supports expanding to other genres. No assumption that the target's current subscribers will transfer to CLIVOX.

## Measuring creator success without violating API terms

Do **not** collect/store giant third-party creator metric tables, calculate forbidden ratios from public YouTube API Data, scrape private retention curves or claim to know their highest-retention second. Record source URL, niche, user-observed topic and evidence label for research; follow YouTube API metadata retention/deletion policy. Rank actual candidate moments using **own media-derived editorial annotations** only after a permitted original source is available. Partner-supplied *authorized* retention evidence must be scoped to that partner and cannot be mixed across unaffiliated owners.
