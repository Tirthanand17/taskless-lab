## Recent official platform evidence

- [YouTube channel monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392): policy separates reused-content/YPP suitability from copyright. **A real written rightsholder license alone cannot guarantee YPP eligibility**; significantly original commentary, storyline, criticism or meaningful modifications are needed. Templated, mass-produced, minimally changed shorts are at risk even when rights are obtained.
- [YouTube Shorts monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220): non-original Shorts and reuploads without original content can produce *ineligible* views for revenue sharing. Eligibility of a particular Short or channel cannot be determined by this test.
- [TeamYouTube, 2026-10-01, prioritizing original Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts): platform update advises own commentary, unique editing, storytelling and personal perspectives; mass reuploads without significant changes may receive less distribution.
- [YouTube copyright tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12361994): copyrighted reused audio/visual material may receive Content ID claims, takedowns and separate rightsholder restrictions. Even creator-signed permission may not cover third-party game publishers, guests and music.

This phase deliberately does not use views, video IDs, retention heatmaps, presumed creator permission or derived virality scores as the proof of content supply.

## Legal, editorial and monetization gates (independent)

1. **Commercial source rights**: human confirms actual rightsholder and signed YouTube redistribution, derivative transformation and monetization permissions; per-source/end-use restrictions, revocation, term, territory, delivery, attribution and credits; separate publisher/game/music/guest clearance.
2. **Substantive CLIVOX creative value**: real editor watches the rights-approved scene and records a distinct original narrative angle, commentary, critical review or educational breakdown. Cheap zooms/captions/emoji alone are not enough. No fake GTA VI events, faked streamer speech, misleading cut context or reused generic interchangeable storyboards.
3. **Exact final video QA and owner release**: one real 1080×1920 master, human watched, audio/caption/crop/rights context verified, exact final hashes, owner's independently verified detached signing identity; recheck revocations immediately before publishing.
4. **Protected upload & feedback**: independently verified correct CLIVOX OAuth, protected private-first uploader, cross-run durable attempt CAS, genuine remote readback and owner approval to schedule. Actual owned Analytics, including legitimate Shorts retention and Studio metrics, may guide a later *human* content experiment; do not derive secret virality from other creators' public stats.
5. **Editorial capacity and sustainability**: verified staff availability, independent review bandwidth, licensing/publisher fees, non-free cloud compute constraints, original commentary effort and rights renewals. Only after a real successful pilot and cleared supply consider increasing from **one carefully reviewed pilot** toward any recurring 2 Shorts + 1 long cadence.

**Human action dependency**: the next high-value real step is P2/P3: select 3–5 authentic rightsholder partnership inquiry routes and obtain a signed, narrowly scoped 1–3-video pilot grant. No outreach is sent by this code or without explicit owner review. Collect actual editor availability and rightsholder-specific scope privately; never put contracts or private tokens in public Git.
